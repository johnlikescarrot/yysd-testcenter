One advantage is reduced water usage – up to 90% less than traditional farming. Also, indoor farms are protected from weather, ensuring year-round harvests. However, reliance on artificial LED lighting increases electricity consumption. If energy comes from fossil fuels, the carbon footprint may exceed that of conventional agriculture.
